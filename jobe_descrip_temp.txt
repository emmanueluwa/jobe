H2 Cognitive Designs (H2CD) is a health technology company working at the intersection of cognitive science, clinical research, and large-scale digital assessment. We build platforms used to measure cognitive performance across clinical trials, occupational health programmes, and large institutional deployments in the UK and internationally.
Our clients include demanding and security-conscious organisations across healthcare, national institutions, and sectors where the performance and reliability of what we build genuinely matter. Our infrastructure is cloud-native, and our engineering team is distributed, collaborative, highly skilled and highly specialised.
We have a rapidly growing portfolio of significant projects, and this role sits at the heart of how we deliver them.
The Role
This is a core engineering position. You will design, develop, and maintain high-quality web and mobile app-based cognitive testing and remote clinical assessment solutions for healthcare providers, academic partners, and related industries.
What You Will Be Doing
Software Design and Development

 Design, develop, and implement high-quality full-stack web applications for cognitive assessment and remote clinical testing
 Write clean, efficient, and well-documented code in Python, Django, JavaScript, HTML, and other relevant languages
 Collaborate with the development team on technical designs and implementation strategies
 Develop and maintain RESTful APIs for integration with other systems
 Implement responsive and accessible user interfaces for web and mobile platforms
 Participate in code reviews to maintain code quality and knowledge sharing

Software Maintenance

 Learn, maintain, and improve existing software codebases as required by customer or company requirements
 Ensure software operability across various devices, browsers, and platforms
 Monitor software performance and implement optimizations as needed
 Respond efficiently to reported software maintenance and defect requests
 Maintain accurate version control and documentation of reported issues and remedial actions taken
 Implement bug fixes and enhancements promptly

Testing and Quality Assurance

 Write and execute unit tests, integration tests, and end-to-end tests
 Identify and resolve software defects and security vulnerabilities
 Ensure that all code meets industry standards for security and data protection
 Apply best practices for test-driven development when appropriate
 Maintain test documentation and test coverage

Professional Development

 Stay current with industry trends, technologies, and best practices
 Participate in team meetings and initiatives to support business growth
 Research and evaluate new technologies that could benefit the company
 Share knowledge and expertise with team members
 Propose innovative solutions to technical challenges
